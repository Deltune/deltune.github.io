How to play?
  1. One person creates a room, the others enter the 4-letter code.
  2. Each round, a theme appears: "Name a fruit".
  3. Type as many answers as you can before time runs out.
  4. Every answer has a rarity: common 100 points, uncommon 300, rare 600, epic 1000, legendary 2000.
  5. Your rarity and points show up live. An answer that isn't on the list is rejected.
